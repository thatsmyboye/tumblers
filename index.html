How to play

      

        Five wheels, six letters each, three rows showing. Drag a wheel up or down, or tap
        the arrows, to turn it one step.
      

      

        Wheels are chained. A chain runs from an open collar — the wheel you turn — to an
        arrowhead on the wheel it drags along. ↑↑ means that wheel turns the same
        way, ↑↓ the opposite way.
      

      

        The strip above the wheels says what each column can do.
        free turns alone.
        ○ drives turns alone and pulls another — nothing pulls it back.
        ▼ pulled still turns on its own but drifts when its partner moves.
        🔒 seized has no arrows at all: it moves only in lockstep with
        whatever drives it.
      

      

        Combination needs the middle row to spell a word. Full Lock needs all three
        rows at once. The Daily is one lock played both ways: crack the Combination, then
        carry on to open the Full Lock for a bonus star.
      

      

        Par is the fewest moves possible. Match it for three stars. Undo is free but
        counted; a hint points at the next move and caps you at two stars. Press and hold any
        wheel to light up what moves with it.
      

      
Keyboard: 1–5 pick a wheel, ← → move, ↑ ↓ turn, Z undo, H hint.
