        How to play
        

          Five wheels, six letters each, three rows showing. Swipe a wheel or use the arrows to
          turn it one step.
        

        

          Wheels are chained. A chain runs from an open collar — the wheel you turn — to an
          arrowhead on the wheel it drags along. ↑↑ means that wheel turns the same
          way, ↑↓ the opposite way.
        

        

          The strip above the wheels says what each column can do.
          free turns alone.
          linked still turns on its own — it just pulls, or gets pulled,
          as well. seized has no arrows at all: it moves only in lockstep
          with whatever drives it, and its chain is drawn as a thick bar-ended coupling.
        

        

          Press and hold any wheel to light up exactly what moves with it. Hold a seized wheel to
          see which wheel you have to turn instead.
        

        

          Combination needs the middle row to spell a word. Full Lock needs all three
          rows at once. Par is the fewest moves possible; undos are free but counted.
