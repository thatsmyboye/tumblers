        How to play
        

          Five wheels, six letters each, three rows showing. Swipe a wheel or use the arrows to
          turn it one step.
        

        

          Wheels are chained: turning one turns another with it. ↑↑ means the linked wheel
          turns the same way, ↑↓ means it turns the opposite way. A seized wheel has
          no arrows — it only moves when something else drags it.
        

        

          Combination needs the middle row to spell a word. Full Lock needs all three
          rows at once. Par is the fewest moves possible; undos are free but counted.
